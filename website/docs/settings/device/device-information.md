# Device Information

The **Device information** group in the device settings shows what the app knows about your monitor. You can't change these values; the app fills them in from the Philips Avent cloud.

* **Model**: the family your monitor belongs to: **SCD973 / SCD923 / SCD921**, **SCD951 / SCD953** or **SenseIQ**. The cloud only reports the family, not the exact model. See [Supported monitors](../../getting-started.md#supported-monitors) for what the family means for your monitor. A monitor the app doesn't know shows *Unknown model*.
* **Product id**: the id the cloud reports for the monitor's family.
* **Firmware**: the monitor's firmware version, and the version of its base firmware.

Mention the model, product id and firmware when you report a problem.

## Locating the Settings

1. Open the **Homey Mobile App** or **Homey Web App**.
2. Navigate to **Devices** and select your baby monitor.
3. Open the device settings:
   * **Mobile App:** Tap the gear icon → **Advanced Settings**
   * **Web App:** Click the gear icon and scroll to **Advanced Settings**
4. Find the **Device information** group.
