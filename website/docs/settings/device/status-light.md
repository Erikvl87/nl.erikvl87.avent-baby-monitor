# Status Light

The **Status light** setting, in the **Monitor** group, turns the small light on the front of the monitor on or off. It is on by default.

Turn it off if the light bothers your baby in a dark room. It doesn't change anything else about the monitor.

Like the alert settings, this is the monitor's own setting: a change in the official app shows up in Homey, and the other way around.

## Locating the Setting

1. Open the **Homey Mobile App** or **Homey Web App**.
2. Navigate to **Devices** and select your baby monitor.
3. Open the device settings:
   * **Mobile App:** Tap the gear icon → **Advanced Settings**
   * **Web App:** Click the gear icon and scroll to **Advanced Settings**
4. Find **Status light** in the **Monitor** group.
