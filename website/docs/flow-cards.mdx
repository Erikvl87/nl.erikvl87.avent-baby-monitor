import driverFlow from '@root/drivers/baby-monitor/driver.flow.compose.json';

export const card = (kind, id) => {
  const found = driverFlow[kind].find((c) => c.id === id);
  if (!found) throw new Error(`Flow card ${kind}/${id} not found in driver.flow.compose.json`);
  return found;
};

# Flow Cards

The **Philips Avent Baby Monitor** app provides trigger cards (When...), condition cards (And...) and action cards (Then...) for your baby monitor. Some come with Homey itself, the others are added by the app.

## Homey's standard cards

Many capabilities of the monitor come with Homey's standard Flow cards. You find them under your baby monitor device, like for any other device.

| Capability | Cards | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Motion alarm | When, And | The alarm turns on, turns off, or is on. |
| Sound detected | When, And | On SenseIQ models, crying counts as a sound too. |
| Temperature | When, And | The temperature changed, with the temperature as a token. |
| Night light and brightness | When, And, Then | Turn the night light on or off, or set its brightness. |
| Lullaby playing, next, previous and volume | When, And, Then | Play or stop, skip, and set the volume. |
| Baby in crib | When, And | SenseIQ only. Shown as the presence alarm. |

:::info

The motion, sound and crying alarms turn themselves off after **30 seconds**. A Flow on "turned off" runs 30 seconds after the last detection, not when your baby settled. See [Capabilities](capabilities.md#sound-detected).

:::

## Triggers (When...)

### Baby started crying

**SenseIQ only.** Runs when the SenseIQ hears your baby cry.

<FlowCard data={card('triggers', 'alarm_crying_true')} />

### Baby stopped crying

**SenseIQ only.** Runs when the crying alarm turns off again, 30 seconds after the last cry.

<FlowCard data={card('triggers', 'alarm_crying_false')} />

### Breathing rate changed

**SenseIQ only.** Runs when the breathing rate changes while your baby is in the crib.

<FlowCard data={card('triggers', 'measure_breathing_rate_changed')} />

Tokens provided:

| Token | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Breaths per minute | Number | The new breathing rate. |

### Privacy mode turned on

Runs when privacy mode is turned on, from Homey or from the official app. The camera is off from then on.

<FlowCard data={card('triggers', 'privacy_mode_true')} />

### Privacy mode turned off

Runs when privacy mode is turned off. The camera is on again.

<FlowCard data={card('triggers', 'privacy_mode_false')} />

### Lullaby changed

Runs when a different sound is selected, from Homey or on the monitor.

<FlowCard data={card('triggers', 'lullaby-changed')} />

Tokens provided:

| Token | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Lullaby | Text | The name of the sound in your language, for example "White Noise". |
| Lullaby id | Text | The id of the sound, for example `white_noise` or `brahms_lullaby`. Handy in logic that shouldn't depend on the language. |

### Monitor went offline

Runs when the monitor loses its connection, for example when it is unplugged or out of Wi-Fi range. The app learns this from the Philips Avent cloud, which can take a few minutes to notice. It doesn't run when only Homey loses its internet connection.

<FlowCard data={card('triggers', 'went-offline')} />

### Monitor came back online

Runs when the monitor is connected again after being offline.

<FlowCard data={card('triggers', 'came-online')} />

## Conditions (And...)

All condition cards can be inverted, for example "Privacy mode isn't on".

### Privacy mode is on

Checks whether privacy mode is on, so the camera is off.

<FlowCard data={card('conditions', 'privacy-mode-is-on')} />

### Lullaby is selected

Checks whether a specific sound is the selected one. It doesn't check whether it is playing; combine it with Homey's "is playing" card for that.

<FlowCard data={card('conditions', 'lullaby-is')} />

### Baby is crying

**SenseIQ only.** Checks whether the crying alarm is on.

<FlowCard data={card('conditions', 'is-crying')} />

## Actions (Then...)

### Play a lullaby

Selects one of the 15 sounds on the monitor and starts playing it.

<FlowCard data={card('actions', 'play-lullaby')} />

### Stop the lullaby after a while

Sets the monitor's own lullaby timer. The monitor stops the lullaby when the time is up, even if Homey is offline by then.

<FlowCard data={card('actions', 'set-lullaby-timer')} />

### Turn the lullaby timer off

Turns the lullaby timer off. The lullaby keeps playing until it is stopped.

<FlowCard data={card('actions', 'cancel-lullaby-timer')} />

### Turn the night light off after a while

Sets the monitor's own night light timer. The monitor turns the night light off when the time is up.

<FlowCard data={card('actions', 'set-night-light-timer')} />

### Turn the night light timer off

Turns the night light timer off. The night light stays on until it is turned off.

<FlowCard data={card('actions', 'cancel-night-light-timer')} />

### Turn privacy mode on or off

Turns the camera off (on) or back on (off).

<FlowCard data={card('actions', 'set-privacy-mode')} />

### Turn motion detection on or off

Turns the monitor's motion detection on or off. This is the same as the **Motion detection** [device setting](settings/device/alerts.md), which follows along.

<FlowCard data={card('actions', 'set-motion-detection')} />

### Turn sound detection on or off

Turns the monitor's sound detection on or off. This is the same as the **Sound detection** [device setting](settings/device/alerts.md), which follows along.

<FlowCard data={card('actions', 'set-sound-detection')} />

## How to Use These Cards

1. In the Homey app, go to **Flows**.
2. Either **edit an existing Flow** or **create a new Flow**:
   * **Mobile App:** Tap the **+** icon in the top-right corner, then select **New Flow**.
   * **Web App:** Click **New Flow** to start a new flow (basic or advanced).
3. Pick the right section: **When** for triggers, **And** for conditions, **Then** for actions.
4. Search for your **baby monitor's device name** (the name you gave it when adding it).
5. Select the Flow card you want and configure the parameters.

### Example: a soft light when your baby wakes up

* **When:** *Sound detected* turned on (your baby monitor)
* **And:** the time is between 22:00 and 07:00
* **Then:** set the night light brightness to 20%, and *Turn the night light off after* 20 minutes
