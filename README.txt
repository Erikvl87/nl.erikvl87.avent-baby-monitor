Bring your Philips Avent connected baby monitor into Homey. Watch the nursery live, get motion and sound alarms within seconds, follow the room temperature, and let Flows take care of the night light and lullabies at bedtime. SenseIQ models also tell you when your baby is in the crib or crying, and how they are breathing.

The app works with the SCD923, SCD951, SCD953, SCD973 and SenseIQ monitors that you set up with the Philips Avent Baby Monitor+ app: sign in with that account and pick your monitors.
