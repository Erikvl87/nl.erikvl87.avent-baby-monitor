Keep an eye on the nursery from Homey. This app connects the Philips Avent connected baby monitors (SCD923, SCD951, SCD953, SCD973 and the SenseIQ models) that you set up with the Philips Avent Baby Monitor+ app: see the room temperature, get motion and sound alarms, and control the night light, the lullabies and the camera's privacy mode. On SenseIQ models you also see whether your baby is in the crib, the breathing rate, and when your baby cries.

Sign in with your Baby Monitor+ account. Philips Avent emails you a verification code to finish signing in; the app keeps the session, not your password. Besides the cloud, the app listens to the monitor on your home network, so alarms arrive within seconds.

This app is not made or supported by Philips. It doesn't show video.

Flow cards: alarms for motion, sound and crying, temperature and breathing rate changes, baby in crib, the lullaby changing, and the monitor going offline or coming back online. Play a lullaby, set the lullaby or night light timer, turn the night light, privacy mode, motion detection and sound detection on or off.
